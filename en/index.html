🫀 HealthOS — your AI health coach

Build and run a personal health coach in a Telegram bot, grounded in your data—wearable, blood, DNA, and diet. From understanding to building to using, with each term explained as it comes up.

What you’ll build

HealthOS is a complete blueprint of a health coach: a Telegram bot with its own database (Supabase) that reads your wearable, understands food photos using vision, remembers your history, and grounds every recommendation in your lab results and genetics.

Instead of generic tips, it gives you advice mechanism-aware: knows the physiological reason in you. The author ran this for 15 days and lost 3,5 kg without going hungry — not by magic, but by seeing what triggers their behavior.

⚠️ Not medical advice

It’s a software blueprint and an example from someone who consulted doctors at every step. AI can hallucinate—verify everything with professionals.

📊 The four sources feed into the coach (Telegram) and come out as specific advice.
